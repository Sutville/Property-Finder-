Sources, definitions and gaps

Research snapshot checked 16 October 2026. Click the source link beside every property, comparable and council row.

  1. All 32 boroughs and the City of London are included. District index is indicative, not a certified ONSPD boundary extract. It is not evidence that every district was independently priced.
  2. ONS rents cover new and existing tenancies and are borough averages, not attainable new-let quotes. City of London figures are suppressed/unavailable on these ONS pages.
  3. ONS flat and terrace sale prices include all bedroom counts. Bedroom-specific sale averages are unavailable; do not divide bedroom rent by these prices as a matched yield.
  4. Budget scenario yields use borough rent divided by the selected hypothetical purchase price. They do not establish that a suitable property is available in that borough.
  5. Listed prices and tenures are advertised facts, not legal verification or an offer. Listings can change or be withdrawn. Confirm availability, complete lease, remaining years at completion and permission to let.
  6. Estimated attainable rents use a small number of asking comparables, not representative district averages or valuations. Size, condition, transport and tenancy restrictions affect the result.
  7. Gross yield = monthly rent × 12 ÷ purchase price. Excludes stamp duty, fees, finance, tax, voids, management, repairs, ground rent and service charges. Known-cost yields subtract only disclosed recurring property charges and are not net yields.
  8. Council rents generally require an inspected property-specific quote. LHA is a benefit benchmark, not a guaranteed council payment. The illustrative BRMA selected for each borough is approximate; obtain the actual BRMA for the full postcode.
  9. Tenant-finding and rent-insurance offers are not council leases. PLA rent may cover occupied periods only. Incentive payments are excluded from recurring yields.
  10. Research is a dated snapshot. No automatic refresh of the whole research dataset is provided. Live URL import, where enabled, retrieves only the selected listing and may be blocked by the source.
